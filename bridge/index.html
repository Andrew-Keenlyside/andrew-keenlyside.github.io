BRIDGE

Brain Reconstruction Integrating Distributed Graph Estimates

Whole-brain structural connectivity in terabyte-scale synchrotron imaging of the human brain.

Developed at UCL in the X-ray Microscopy Group of the NIH BRAIN CONNECTS Center for Large-scale Imaging of Neural Circuits (LINC). The code is private until publication; the format and viewers it writes to are open now.

BRIDGE on GitHub Open tools
