Andrew Keenlyside

I study human white-matter connectivity in terabyte-scale synchrotron imaging of whole brains, and build open tools for data at that size.

PhD researcher, UCL Mechanical Engineering and UCL Hawkes Institute. X-ray Microscopy Group, NIH BRAIN CONNECTS Center for Large-scale Imaging of Neural Circuits (LINC).

Code on GitHub Software & data

About

I trained in medicine before physics: three years of the MBChB at Dundee, then an intercalated BSc in Medical Physics and Biomedical Engineering at UCL (First Class Honours, Dean’s List). I am now doing a PhD on research leave from medicine, in the X-ray Microscopy Group of the NIH BRAIN CONNECTS LINC centre.

Research

Multiscale connectomics in terabyte-scale synchrotron imaging

PhD, 2024–. UCL Hawkes Institute and NIH BRAIN CONNECTS (LINC)

White-matter connectivity in HiP-CT scans of whole human brains, as part of the NIH BRAIN CONNECTS Center for Large-scale Imaging of Neural Circuits.

BRIDGE

Zarr Vectors

2026–, with the Allen Institute

An open Python toolkit for the Zarr Vectors specification: chunked, multiscale storage for streamlines, graphs and meshes, so network analysis scales to terabyte-sized connectomes.
